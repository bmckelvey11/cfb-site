Offense is points per possession above the league average; + is better. Defense is points per possession allowed against the average; − is better. Volume is possessions per team per game against the average; + is more. Long scoring drives use up possessions, so an efficient offense can rank low here. Each cell shows the rank, the rating, and the move since the previous week.

Ratings adjust for opponents and are pulled toward league average until a team has several games: a team's own results get half the weight after about three games for offense and defense and eight for volume. Grey rows have fewer than three games. Method: ridge_v1, λ 40 possessions and 8 games, fit on completed FBS-vs-FBS regular-season games.
