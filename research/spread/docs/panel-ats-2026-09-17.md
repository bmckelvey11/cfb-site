# Does any single panel model have an ATS edge? — 2026-09-17

**EXPLORATORY.** Not registered in `prereg-line-movement.md`. Written because
`panel-vs-line-2026-09-17.md` leaned on RMSE, and **RMSE is a weak test of bettability**.

## Why this study exists

The RMSE study found no panel column beats the market line. That is not the same as "no column
is bettable", and the earlier document framed it too strongly. RMSE is squared, symmetric, and
averaged over every game, so it is dominated by the middle of the distribution. A bet needs only
the **sign** of the disagreement to be right, only on the **games you choose**, and only
**52.381%** of the time. A model can be worse on average and still profitable on a subset.

So this asks the betting question directly, per model: bet the side the model favours when it
disagrees with the line by at least a threshold.

## Answer: the broad null holds — but one model shows exactly the pattern RMSE would have hidden.

### The sweep

141 models × thresholds {1, 2, 3} points, cells with ≥ 200 bets. Season-cluster bootstrap SE,
Benjamini-Hochberg q-values across the whole family (`base.bh_qvalues`), pushes dropped.

| | value |
|---|---|
| cells with ≥ 200 bets | 436 (148 models) |
| of those, with ≥ 3 season clusters (a valid SE) | **330** |
| testable cells above break-even | **2 of 330** |
| surviving BH q < 0.10 | **1** |
| pooled ATS across testable cells | **0.4981** |
| median cell ATS | 0.4980 |

The panel as a whole sits at coin-flip minus vig, exactly as the RMSE study implied. 106 cells
covered one or two seasons and have no valid cluster SE; they are reported but cannot be tested.

### The survivor, and why it matters

`linecrunch`, at a disagreement threshold of 3 points:

| | value |
|---|---|
| bets | 1,185 |
| ATS | **0.5418** |
| cluster-bootstrap SE | 0.0055 |
| z vs break-even | 3.28 |
| BH q | **0.0026** |
| **RMSE ratio to the line** | **1.032 — 3.2% WORSE than the line** |

It is consistent across every season it existed, and monotone in threshold:

| threshold | bets | ATS | 2015 | 2016 | 2017 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≥ 1 pt | 1,803 | 0.5225 | 0.5218 | 0.5271 | 0.5186 |
| ≥ 2 pts | 1,468 | 0.5266 | 0.5305 | 0.5311 | 0.5182 |
| **≥ 3 pts** | 1,185 | **0.5418** | 0.5530 | 0.5294 | 0.5427 |

Two things are worth naming. **The edge grows with disagreement** — the opposite of `phcover`,
where beat-close *fell* as edge rose, which was the signature of no information. And **all three
seasons clear break-even**, so it is not one season carrying a pooled number.

**This is the concrete vindication of the objection to the RMSE framing.** `linecrunch` is worse
than the market line on RMSE and would have been discarded by that ranking, yet it is the one
cell in 330 with a defensible ATS record. Accuracy of the point estimate and sign-edge at a
threshold are different properties, and the panel contains at least one column where they come
apart.

## Why it still is not a bet

- **`linecrunch` is dead.** It published for three seasons, 2015–2017, and is **not in today's
  live PT snapshot**. Nothing here can be traded forward.
- **Three season clusters is the degenerate floor.** `MIN_CLUSTERS` is 3, so the SE computed at
  all — but a bootstrap over three seasons cannot capture between-season variance, and 0.0055 is
  certainly too small. The per-season consistency above is better evidence than the z.
- **It was selected from 436 cells.** BH controls the false-discovery rate across the family, and
  q = 0.0026 is a real correction, not a raw p. But the result is still in-sample and pooled: the
  honest status is **a hypothesis worth a walk-forward test**, not an edge. `prior_skill()` and
  `walk_forward()` are the registered machinery for that, and they cannot be run on a model that
  stopped publishing.

## What this does not support

- **Not a retraction of the RMSE study.** Its measurements stand; the correction is to its
  framing, which treated RMSE ranking as sufficient to dismiss bettability. It is not, and
  `panel-vs-line-2026-09-17.md` now carries a pointer here.
- **Not a claim the panel is bettable.** 2 of 330 testable cells above break-even, pooled 0.4981.
  The broad null is if anything reinforced by testing it the right way.
- **Not a version A or B read.** Amendment B3's stopping rule is untouched.
- **Not a statement about E4**, which forecasts line *movement* from the opener, a different
  estimand.

## Method

Data: `data/ingest/prediction_tracker_lines.csv`, all seasons, `match_status == 'matched'`,
margins oriented to PT's home team by `base.load()`. `MARKET_LINES` excluded.

The model implies a home margin of `-model`, the market `-line`, so the model favours **home**
iff `model - line < 0`. Home covers iff `y + line > 0`; pushes dropped. Cells need ≥ 200 bets
(`--min-bets`) and ≥ 3 season clusters for a valid SE.

```bash
python research/spread/scripts/eval_panel_ats.py
```

Writes `data/processed/panel_ats.json`, including every cell with its q-value.
