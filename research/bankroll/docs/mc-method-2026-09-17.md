# Method: how the bankroll projection is computed

Written 2026-09-17 for outside review. Self-contained: every constant, distribution,
and decision rule the projection uses is stated here, with the code path it lives on.
Reviewers should be able to reimplement it from this document alone and reproduce the
headline numbers to within Monte Carlo noise.

Code: `research/bankroll/scripts/mc_combined_totals.py` (the simulator),
`bankroll_config_sweep.py` (the unit sweep and decision rule),
`pooled_growth_chart.py` (fan charts), `bankroll_stress.py` (the skeptical-model
stress tests). Results are in
[seed-bankroll-proposal-2026-09-17.md](seed-bankroll-proposal-2026-09-17.md);
the stress results and the disposition of the outside review's items are in
[bankroll-stress-2026-09-17.md](bankroll-stress-2026-09-17.md).

Revised the same day after review: bust rates reported as counts, stake notation
made per-leg, the 58.2% planning number's provenance stated, the over-zero count
mixture stated, the outcome-correlation figure labeled an approximation.

---

## 1. Question

Starting from a $20,000 bankroll before week 4 of the 2026 college football season,
betting two totals strategies for twelve weeks (weeks 4–15, September 24 to
December 12), what is the distribution of the ending bankroll, and what unit size
should be used?

## 2. Inputs

### 2.1 The two legs

| leg | side | price | graded record used as the prior | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| over-zero | OVER | −120 | 151–83, walk-forward 2016–2025 | `models/over_zero/docs/ROI_HITRATE.md` |
| Greenline | UNDER (~85%) | −110 | see 2.2 | `research/totals/docs/greenline-season-review-2026-09-16.md` |

Net payout on a win, per unit staked: `b = 100 / |price|`. So b = 0.8333 at −120,
b = 0.9091 at −110. Break-even win rate `1 / (1 + b)`: 54.55% at −120, 52.38% at −110.

### 2.2 Greenline priors

Two records are carried everywhere and the answer is reported as a bracket.

| prior | record | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `n49` | 27–22 | 2026 week 2 Greenline totals flags, graded at PFF's captured line |
| `pooled` | 141–109 | n49 plus 201 personal full-game NCAAF unders, 2023-08 to 2025-12, 114–87 at mean price −110.1 |

The 201 personal unders were mostly Greenline flags in earlier seasons, so pooling is
treated as prior evidence for the same signal, not as independent confirmation. They
also sat at a higher median total (58.5) than the 2026 flags (52.5), so the pooled
rate probably reads high. Neither prior is selected; both are run.

### 2.3 Win-rate uncertainty

Each simulated path draws its own true win rate from the Jeffreys posterior of the
record:

    p ~ Beta(wins + 0.5, losses + 0.5)

| leg / prior | posterior mean | posterior sd | P(p below break-even) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| over-zero, raw | 64.5% | 0.031 | ~0% |
| over-zero, after haircut (used) | 58.2% | 0.031 | 12% |
| Greenline `n49` | 55.0% | 0.070 | 35% |
| Greenline `pooled` | 56.4% | 0.031 | 10% |

**Over-zero haircut.** The bias threshold that defines an over-zero bet (1.75) was
chosen on the same 2016–2025 data, so its 64.5% is selection-inflated. The model
guide's planning number is 58.2%: the lower endpoint of the 95% Wilson score interval
on the raw 151–83 record (`ROI_HITRATE.md`, [58.21%, 70.38%]). It is a conservative
planning estimate, not a bound. The simulator subtracts the difference (0.063) from
every path's draw and clips to [0.01, 0.99]. This shifts the posterior mean, not its
spread; `oz_extra_sd` and `oz_center` in the stress harness make the correction
itself uncertain and larger, and the decision does not move (stress doc, item 1).

### 2.4 Volume

**Greenline (default, `--gl-volume range`).** Each week's number of unders is drawn
uniformly on the closed interval [6, 12], then capped at that week's FBS-vs-FBS slate.
Greenline flags every FBS-vs-FBS game (week 2: 49 games, 49 flags; week 3: 57 and
57), so the slate is the ceiling. Slate per week, weeks 4–15, from `core.fact_game`
for 2026 (weeks 14–15 from 2025 because 2026 is not scheduled that far):

    58, 56, 58, 59, 56, 56, 62, 67, 66, 65, 67, 9

Expected Greenline bets over the span: 11 × 9 + E[min(U{6..12}, 9)] where
E[min] = (6+7+8+9+9+9+9)/7 = 8.14, total 107.1. (A reviewer's 107.3 used 58/7; the
sum is 57.)

Two alternative volume modes exist for sensitivity: `slate` (Poisson around
coverage × slate, coverage 13% by default, the rate the 201-bet record was earned at)
and `constant` (Poisson around coverage × 49).

**Over-zero.** The season's remaining count is drawn once per path as a mixture,
not a single Poisson: `λ` is sampled uniformly from the week-4-and-later counts of
2021–2025, (8, 11, 9, 11, 14), then `N ~ Poisson(λ)`. Mean 10.6; variance
E[λ] + Var(λ) = 10.6 + 4.24, larger than a plain Poisson(10.6) because the
between-season draw is kept. Those N bets are then thinned uniformly across the
remaining weeks: in week w (0-indexed), `n_w ~ Binomial(remaining, 1 / (12 − w))`.

### 2.5 Correlation between bets on the same slate

Bets in the same week share one scoring-environment shock through a Gaussian copula.

For each week, draw `Z ~ N(0, 1)` once per path. Each bet has a latent
`S = √ρ · Z + √(1−ρ) · ε`, with `ε ~ N(0, 1)` independent per bet, so `S ~ N(0, 1)`
and any two bets in the week have latent correlation ρ.

Thresholds from the path's win rate p:

- UNDER wins when `S < z_under`, with `z_under = Φ⁻¹(p)`.
- OVER wins when `S > z_over`, with `z_over = Φ⁻¹(1 − p)`.

Conditional on Z the bets are independent, so the week's wins are Binomial:

    q_under = Φ((z_under − √ρ · Z) / √(1−ρ))        wins ~ Binomial(n, q_under)
    q_over  = 1 − Φ((z_over − √ρ · Z) / √(1−ρ))     wins ~ Binomial(n, q_over)

The Binomial trick is what makes 100k paths × ~120 bets cheap: no per-bet draws.

Because over-zero is OVER and Greenline is mostly UNDER, the same Z pushes them in
opposite directions. A high-scoring Saturday helps one leg and hurts the other.

**ρ = 0.10 is assumed, not estimated.** One graded week cannot measure it. ρ is the
latent (tetrachoric) correlation; the induced correlation between binary outcomes is
smaller. The figure `ρ · φ(z)² / (p(1−p))` ≈ 0.063 at p = 0.56 is a first-order
approximation from the threshold model for two same-side bets at the same p, not an
exact relationship and not the cross-leg figure. The reproducible definition is the
simulation itself. ρ ∈ {0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.35, 0.50} is run in the stress doc.

### 2.6 Staking

**Default: weekly re-sizing.** At the start of each week, with
`B_w⁺ = max(bankroll_0 + cumulative_pnl, 0)`,

    stake_GL,w = B_w⁺ × 0.005        stake_OZ,w = B_w⁺ × 0.01

Two distinct dollar stakes from the same beginning-of-week bank. Within a week every bet is the
same size, because Saturday kickoffs are simultaneous and results cannot be reacted
to. A path at or below zero stakes nothing.

**Alternative: flat (`--flat-stakes`).** `stake_leg = bankroll_0 × unit_leg` all
season. A path can go through zero and keep betting; the bust rate is reported so
those percentiles can be discounted.

Week P&L: `wins × stake × b − (n − wins) × stake`, summed over both legs.

No stop-loss, no per-bet Kelly, no compounding within a week.

### 2.7 Not modeled

- **Pushes.** All 234 graded over-zero bets, all 49 graded Greenline flags, and all
  201 personal unders sat on half-point lines. Realized push rate zero.
- **Price variation.** Every Greenline bet is at −110, every over-zero at −120.
- **Conflicts.** A game flagged on opposite sides by both legs is bet once, by
  Greenline. Rare so far; not simulated.
- **Line shopping, limits, availability.**

## 3. Outputs

Per configuration, from N paths (100,000 for headline runs, 50,000 per sweep cell):

- ending bankroll percentiles 1/5/10/25/50/75/90/95/99
- P(end below start), P(end below 75% of start), P(end above 125% of start)
- worst single week: median and 5th percentile of each path's minimum weekly P&L
- busts: count of paths whose cumulative P&L ever reached −bankroll_0, out of N.
  "0" means 0 of N; the rule-of-three 95% upper bound at N = 50,000 is 0.006%, under
  the model
- maximum drawdown from each path's running peak; weeks ending below the start;
  expected shortfall at 5% (mean of the worst 5% of endings)
- mean turnover (total dollars staked)
- fan: percentiles 5/25/50/75/95 of the bankroll after each week
- P(drawn win rate below break-even), per leg

Seed 20260917 throughout. Percentiles are per week, not per path, so the fan chart
bands are marginal envelopes, not sample trajectories.

## 4. Decision rule for the unit size

Grid: Greenline unit ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0}% × prior ∈ {pooled, n49}, over-zero
fixed at 1%, volume 6–12 a week, weekly re-sizing. 10 cells.

**(a) Constraint:** P(end below 75% of start) ≤ 1% and zero busts observed in
50,000 paths, required under **both** priors. At the 1% boundary the binomial
standard error is ≈ 0.045 points per cell.
**(b) Downside ratio:** `(median − start) / (median − 5th percentile)`, reported as a
column; higher is better.

Recommended = the unit with the highest mean-of-priors median among cells passing (a).

At 0.5%: P(down) 26.0% pooled / 36.9% n49; worst week median −$534 / −$551; turnover
~$13,100. Over-zero alone contributes a median of about +$133.

Analytic check on the spread. Per-bet sd at −110 and p ≈ 0.56 is ≈ 0.95 units. A
9-bet week at ρ = 0.10 has sd ≈ 0.95 × √9 × √(1 + 8 × 0.063) ≈ 3.5 units ≈ $350 at
$100 units. Twelve independent weeks: ≈ $1,200. Adding the pooled win-rate sd (0.031
× 107 bets × ~1.9 units swing ≈ 6.3 units ≈ $630): ≈ $1,360 total. The simulated
5th–95th half-width at 0.5% is ≈ $2,470, i.e. sd ≈ $1,500. Consistent given the
re-sizing and the Poisson over-zero leg.

## 6. Things a reviewer should challenge

1. **Beta posterior as the win-rate model.** Jeffreys prior on a record that is
   itself selected (over-zero threshold; personal picks). Is a haircut on the mean
   enough, or should the sd be inflated too?
2. **Pooling.** The 201 personal unders are the same signal in earlier seasons, at a
   ~6-point higher median total. Is a 141–109 pooled record the right way to carry
   that, or should it be a hierarchical prior with a between-population variance?
3. **Latent vs outcome correlation.** ρ = 0.10 on the latent normal gives ~0.063
   between outcomes. Is that the right order of magnitude for same-day college
   totals? Is one shared factor per week enough, or should Thursday/Friday/Saturday
   be separate?
4. **Opposite-side correlation.** The model makes over-zero OVERs and Greenline
   UNDERs negatively correlated through the shared shock. That assumes the shock is
   "scoring environment". If it is "market mispricing direction" the sign is wrong.
5. **Volume.** 6–12 a week is a plan. The record the pooled rate came from was ~13%
   of flags; 9 a week is ~16%. Does the win rate transfer to the extra 3%?
6. **Weekly re-sizing with within-week flat stakes.** Is that the operational
   reality, and is the max(·, 0) floor the right treatment of a path at zero?
7. **Uniform thinning of over-zero.** Real over-zero volume is front-loaded; is
   uniform across weeks 4–15 a fair spread of its ~11 remaining bets?
8. **The decision rule.** P(−25%) ≤ 1% under both priors is a stated tolerance, not
   derived. Kelly-fractional sizing off the posterior would give a different answer;
   the model guide's quarter-Kelly off 58.2% is ~3% for over-zero.
9. **Percentile fan vs path fan.** Per-week percentiles are not trajectories; a
   reader can misread the band as a path envelope.
10. **Pushes at zero.** Half-point lines so far; any move to whole-number totals
    would need a push branch.

## 8. Pseudocode

```
for each of N paths:
    p_oz  = Beta(151.5, 83.5) - 0.063, clipped to [0.01, 0.99]
    p_gl  = Beta(w + 0.5, l + 0.5)              # (141, 109) pooled or (27, 22) n49
    z_oz  = Phi_inv(1 - p_oz)                   # OVER wins when S > z_oz
    z_gl  = Phi_inv(p_gl)                       # UNDER wins when S < z_gl
    N_oz  = Poisson(choice(8, 11, 9, 11, 14))
    bank  = 20000
    for w in 0..11:
        Z      = Normal(0, 1)                   # this week's shared shock
        stake_gl = max(bank, 0) * 0.005;  stake_oz = max(bank, 0) * 0.01   # flat mode: 20000 * unit
        n_gl   = min(Uniform{6..12}, slate[w])
        n_oz   = Binomial(N_oz_remaining, 1 / (12 - w));  N_oz_remaining -= n_oz
        q_gl   = Phi((z_gl - sqrt(rho) * Z) / sqrt(1 - rho))
        q_oz   = 1 - Phi((z_oz - sqrt(rho) * Z) / sqrt(1 - rho))
        w_gl   = Binomial(n_gl, q_gl);  w_oz = Binomial(n_oz, q_oz)
        pnl    = w_gl * stake_gl * 0.9091 - (n_gl - w_gl) * stake_gl
               + w_oz * stake_oz * 0.8333 - (n_oz - w_oz) * stake_oz
        bank  += pnl
        record min weekly pnl, min bank, bank percentiles
report percentiles of bank, P(bank < 20000), P(bank < 15000), P(min bank <= 0), turnover
```

A ~40-line numpy version of the Greenline leg alone, with a variance-mixing shortcut
for the correlation instead of the copula, gives a median of $20,798 and a 5–95 band
of $18,376–$23,438 at the same inputs. The ~$150 gap to the full model is the missing
over-zero leg.
