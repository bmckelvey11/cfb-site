# Postseason for the 6 `season_week` endpoints

`260828-j8s` closed the `seasonType` gap for the 13 SEASON-mode endpoints and deliberately
left these six on `regular`: `plays`, `play_stats`, `ppa_players_games`,
`game_player_stats`, `game_team_stats`, `player_success_game`. Postseason week numbering
restarts at 1, so a single `both` call merges regular week 1 with postseason week 1
(`game_team_stats` 2024 wk1: regular=137, postseason=50, both=187, union exact), and
`{name}_{season}_wk{week}.json` has no axis to hold them apart.

Fix: two passes, each asking for one season type, each writing its own filename.

## Probe (2026-08-28, before any edit)

```
postseason weeks per season
  2014: 44 games, weeks {1}
  2019: 46 games, weeks {1}
  2024: 54 games, weeks {1}
  2025: 86 games, weeks {1: 54, 13: 24, 14: 8}     <-- NOT week 1 only

all six endpoints answer for postseason week 1 (2024):
  game_team_stats 50, game_player_stats 50, plays 8812,
  play_stats 2000, ppa_players_games 937, player_success_game 526
```

**2025 is the reason this cannot hardcode `week=1`.** Division II/III playoffs begin in
mid-November, landing in weeks 13-14 — the same fixtures behind the 34 chronology cases
recorded in `docs/data-coverage.md`. Assuming week 1 would silently drop 32 games.

## Design

- `_postseason_weeks(data_dir, season)` reads the already-scraped `games_{season}.json`,
  filters to `seasonType == "postseason"`, returns `sorted(set(week))`. Seed-driven, same
  spirit as the `per_game` game-id seed. Missing seed file -> no postseason pass for that
  season (skip, don't error).
- `_scrape_season_week` runs one pass per season type. Regular keeps
  `{name}_{season}_wk{week}.json`; postseason writes
  `{name}_{season}_post_wk{week}.json`. The two never share a file.
- `--season-type` becomes meaningful for these six again and is symmetric:
  `regular` -> regular only, `postseason` -> postseason only, `both` -> both.
