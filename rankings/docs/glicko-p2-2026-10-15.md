## Method

- **Base.** P1 exactly as frozen (`FROZEN_P1`: σ 15, τ 1.5, w 0.7, δ 6, C = ∞, H 2, u₀ 14),
  P1's game pool (every completed D-I game from the raw CFBD files), and its conference-mean
  offseason targets. P1 was not re-tuned. A check asserts that P2 with neutral settings
  reproduces P1's forecasts exactly.
- **P2's offseason step** adds two preseason features and a feature-dependent persistence:

$$
\begin{gathered}
r_{\text{new}} = m + \operatorname{clamp}(w + c\,z_{rp},\,0,\,1)\,(r_{\text{old}} - m) + b_{rp}\,z_{rp} + b_{rec}\,z_{rec} \\[1em]
\begin{array}{rl}
\text{where}\quad r_{\text{old}}, r_{\text{new}}: & \text{team rating before/after the offseason, points vs average FBS, neutral field} \\
m: & \text{the team's offseason target (its conference mean, P1's rule), points} \\
w: & \text{persistence, unitless (P1: 0.7)} \\
z_{rp}: & \text{z-score of CFBD returning production (percentPPA) among that season's FBS teams} \\
z_{rec}: & \text{z-score of the mean recruiting points of classes } s-3..s \text{ among FBS teams} \\
c, b_{rp}, b_{rec}: & \text{tuned coefficients; } c \text{ unitless, } b \text{ in points per SD}
\end{array}
\end{gathered}
$$

  A team with average returning production and recruiting moves exactly as in P1. With
  $b_{rp} = 3$, a team returning one SD more production than average starts 3 points higher.
  FCS teams and FBS teams missing a row get $z = 0$, so they regress exactly as in P1.
  Talent (`talent_*.json`) was not used: it is missing 2013–14 and changes from FBS+FCS to
  FBS-only in 2024, so its z-scores mean different things across eras.
- **Robust clip.** Before the Kalman step, the innovation (margin − forecast) is clipped at
  clip × √S. This limits how far one surprise blowout moves a team, unlike v1's hard margin
  cap, which also clipped expected blowouts.
- **σ_pred.** The forecast SD is $\sqrt{u_h^2 + u_a^2 + \sigma_{pred}^2}$; the gain keeps
  σ = 15. Tuned separately for P1 and P2 on 11.0–16.0 in steps of 0.5.
- **Selection:** lowest mean CRPS on FBS-vs-FBS regular-season games, 2014–2019 (2013 is
  burn-in). Grid: $b_{rp}$ ∈ {0, 1, 2, 3}, $b_{rec}$ ∈ {0, 1.5, 3, 4.5}, c ∈ {0, 0.05, 0.1},
  w ∈ {0.6, 0.7, 0.8}, clip ∈ {∞, 3, 2.5, 2}.
- **Scoring:** 2021–2025 FBS-vs-FBS regular season, 3,730 games (2020 skipped, as P1 did).
  **This is the third look at this window** for the Glicko family (v1, P1, now P2).
  Paired intervals use a season-week block bootstrap, 2,000 draws.
- **Holdout:** one read of 2026 weeks 1–7, 333 FBS-vs-FBS games, P2 minus P1 paired CRPS and
  MAE only. No market data was read, so P1's sealed confirmation (its encompassing β against
  the open) is untouched.
- **Declared adopt rule:** 2021–25 CRPS improves (interval below 0), MAE not worse, 2026 CRPS
  difference ≤ 0, and the CRPS improvement holds in every one-step stress neighbour.

## Why the prior failed, and what that suggests

The prior beat P1 by 0.065 CRPS in tuning (2014–19) and lost 0.050 on 2021–25. That pattern —
a real in-sample gain that reverses later — is consistent with the preseason signal weakening
after 2020. The transfer portal and extra COVID eligibility plausibly broke the link between
CFBD's returning-production percentage, last year's recruiting classes, and next year's
roster. This doc does not test that explanation. A portal-aware prior (incoming transfer
production, not only returning production) would be the next rung. It would need its own
pre-2021 tuning and a fresh confirmation, and 2021–25 cannot serve as a clean test again.

## Other audit notes (not changed)

- **Realigned teams regress toward their old conference.** At the offseason a team's target
  is the conference it played in last season, because its new label arrives with its first
  game. Texas and Oklahoma regressed toward the 2023 Big 12 mean before 2024, not the SEC's.
  This is a defensible choice (last season's peers), not a leak. Not tested here.
- **Home edge is a single 2.0 points for 2013–2026.** Not re-tuned. Testing an era-varying
  edge would be a new trial.
- **Trial count.** 576 prior-grid + 22 σ_pred + 8 stress + 1 scoring run + 1 holdout read =
  608. Cumulative on the Glicko family: 18,014 + 608 = **18,622**.

## What this does not support

- **Any betting or ATS claim.** No market data was read; the σ_pred change is about calibrated
  probabilities and intervals, not an edge.
- **That preseason information is useless.** Only this construction failed: CFBD's
  percentPPA plus four-year recruiting points, additive, on P1's offseason step.
- **A clean holdout for σ_pred.** Its 2021–25 gain is a third look at that window; its 2026
  effect was not read (the declared holdout read was P2 − P1 only). It changes no means, so
  P1's sealed confirmation is unaffected.
