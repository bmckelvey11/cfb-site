How to read these results
Record and hit rate
Wins, losses, pushes. Hit rate excludes pushes.
Money won and ROI
Historical profit using the tool's $100 unit display. ROI divides profit by total stake.
Break-even and edge
Break-even is the win rate needed to cover the odds. Edge is observed hit rate minus break-even.
Wilson CI
A 95% confidence interval for hit rate; wider intervals mean less precision.
p-value and permutation p
Tests for a winning edge under their null assumptions. Small values are evidence, not the probability a system will win in future. Trying many systems increases false discoveries.
ROI t-stat
Profit relative to its estimated sampling uncertainty.
MDE
Minimum detectable effect: the approximate edge this sample can reliably detect under the model's assumptions.
Clusters and ICC
Clusters group games by season and week. ICC measures similarity of returns within those groups.
Effective N
Estimated independent sample size after accounting for within-group similarity.
Cluster ROI CI
A 95% interval for ROI accounting for season/week groups. An interval spanning zero does not distinguish a gain from a loss.
Streaks
Longest consecutive winning and losing runs in this sample.
V1 Over Probability
A model estimate for the over, not an observed win rate. Open the filter for source and coverage details.
Pregame history
To-date havoc and NGT defensive stats average earlier completed games in the same season, with a 24-hour lag. NGT excludes garbage time. Core ratings and coach style use prior-season information. Missing history stays blank.
