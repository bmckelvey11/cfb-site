## Findings resolved September 11

1. WARNING — Mobile season metrics stacked four tall rows, delaying access to picks.
   Changed to a two-column grid on phones; four columns remain on larger screens.
2. WARNING — At 768px, the wide table hid Bet to behind horizontal scrolling.
   Cards now remain active below 1280px; desktop retains the full table.
3. WARNING — Spread inputs were approximately 30px high and 14px text; selectors
   and reset actions also offered small targets. Made controls at least 44px high,
   input/select text 16px, added stronger borders and visible keyboard focus.
4. WARNING — Bet to label and editor crowded narrow metric cells. Gave its card
   section two columns and allowed its content to wrap without overlap.
5. WARNING — "At or above" and ≥ contradicted the model's strict threshold.
   Changed to "above" and > consistently on the board.
6. WARNING — "Market total" mislabeled totals retained from a qualifying book.
   Renamed to "Signal total" and explained the distinction. Market spread remains
   the actual market spread; the editable scenario can start at the qualifying
   book's spread.
7. WARNING — At 320px, MyBookie.ag and a long summary label overflowed metric cards.
   Reduced horizontal padding, allowed label wrapping and sized Source separately.
8. WARNING — Long sort captions truncated at narrow widths. Replaced with
   Earliest kickoff, Latest kickoff, Highest bias, Lowest bias.
9. WARNING — Invalid spread feedback lacked input error semantics. Added
   aria-invalid, input instructions and polite announcement of changed cutoff.

sections and small secondary metadata are retained, rather than redesigning the
site around a different content hierarchy. No new packages were introduced.
