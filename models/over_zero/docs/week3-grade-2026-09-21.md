# Over-zero week 3 board — graded

**Question.** Did the OVER picks published on the board for the 2026-09-19 slate
win, and where does that leave the season record?

**Method.** `python models/over_zero/scripts/build_weekly_results.py`. Each game is
graded at its **first published signal total**, so a line that moved after
publication does not change a recorded result, and a game that appeared on more
than one board is counted once. Week 3 publications added to `PUBLICATIONS`:
`4f65acb` (2026-09-14 17:44 ET, three picks) and `9838f6a` (2026-09-17 08:59 ET,
two more). The later `79a0b88` and `83bb5dd` boards introduced no new games.
Finals come from CFBD (`/games?year=2026&seasonType=regular`, pulled 2026-09-21);
the raw response is snapshotted to `$CFB_DATA_ROOT/processed/over_zero/results/`.

**Data.** 5 picks, all CFBD week 3, all played 2026-09-19/20. All five settled.

## What this does and does not support

- **One week of five picks is not a measurement.** The 95% interval on 4-1 runs
  37.6% to 96.4%. Break-even (52.4%) is inside it. Nothing about week 3 alone is
  evidence in either direction.
- **The season number is better but still not a result.** 21-9 has a 95% interval
  of 52.1% to 83.3%. Break-even sits just below the floor, which is suggestive and
  nothing more — 30 picks is roughly a third of what it takes to separate a true
  60% from break-even at conventional power.
- **These are not walk-forward folds.** `docs/model-evaluation-standard.md` asks
  for a proper score against the same-time de-vigged market and for folds; this
  doc reports neither. It is a settled-bet record of a published board, which is
  a different and weaker thing than forecast skill. Do not quote the 70% as model
  performance.
- **The slate is not neutral.** Week 3's board was five FBS-vs-lower-tier
  blowout-shaped games, which is what the floor-bias signal selects for. Records
  from such a board should not be extrapolated to conference weeks.
- **Pick count is falling** (14 → 11 → 5) as the 50-point spread cap and the
  playability filter bite. Expect the season interval to tighten slowly.

## Reproduce

```bash
python models/over_zero/scripts/build_weekly_results.py
```

Writes `models/over_zero/site/lib/weekly-results.json`, which is what the site's
settled-results drawers render. Adding a future week means appending its
publication revisions to `PUBLICATIONS` in that script.
