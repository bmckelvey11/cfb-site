P(middle) is an upper bound. Expected remaining points are estimated from the live total and clock, which is a weaker signal than the live market itself (it cannot see possession, observed pace, or a weather turn). The residual spread is therefore wider than reality, which puts too much mass inside the middle window and overstates both the probability and the EV.
